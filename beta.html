How to use this lab

Pokémon: A hand without a Basic Pokémon is a mandatory mulligan. The tool reshuffles and deals seven again until a Basic is found; it does not simulate the opponent's optional extra draws. Magic: Uses the standard London mulligan for a 60-card two-player game: redraw seven, then choose one additional card to bottom per mulligan before keeping. Multiplayer and format-specific exceptions are outside scope.

Coach heuristic: For Magic, the 2–4 land opening is a simple yardstick—not a universal keep rule. For Pokémon, a Basic plus an Energy is a convenience metric, not an official requirement. Both can be misleading for ramp, toolbox, and unusual lists. Imported card types must be accurately classified for reliable statistics.

Welcome to the V8 practice studio

This is an original, independent fan-made personal practice simulator. Inspired by the convenience of tabletop testing and card search, not affiliated with Pokémon, Wizards, Limitless, TCGdex or Scryfall.

What you can do

Switch Pokémon ↔ Magic, search card prints online, build and export decks, save them in this browser, test opening hands, draw, move cards, track zones, and rehearse turns. The integrated Strategic Coach ranks supported moves; the V8 Consistency Lab simulates opening hands and compares deck builds, with Guided, Challenge and Demonstration styles. Pokémon also includes the focused Exeggutor Autoplay coach from V2.

What's intentionally simplified

The custom-deck tabletop is a partial rules engine, not a universal rules judge: common actions, basic timing, attachments, and knockout/death cleanup are enforced. Advanced spells, targets, replacement effects, and most card-specific abilities still require manual resolution. Pokémon's separate coach automates a narrower set of modeled effects. Card legality is only checked for simple counts, not rotating sets, bans or print legality.

Offline and privacy

The studio UI, offline starter lists, hand simulator, and manual tabletop live in one HTML file. Real card images and catalog searches load over HTTPS while online; if unavailable, the cards display polished text placeholders. Saved decks use this browser's local storage; your data never goes to our own server. File-browser storage policies vary, so export your decks to back them up.
